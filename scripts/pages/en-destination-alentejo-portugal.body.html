Why visit Alentejo, Portugal

Alentejo is one of Portugal's most respected wine regions, with historic estates, vineyards as far as the eye can see, and a gastronomy rooted in local tradition. It's an ideal itinerary for those seeking wine tourism without giving up comfort.

With Anne Ferri's training in Gastronomy and Sommellerie, ATF Travel builds wine-tourism itineraries in Alentejo with visits to selected wineries, tastings and boutique stays among the vineyards.
