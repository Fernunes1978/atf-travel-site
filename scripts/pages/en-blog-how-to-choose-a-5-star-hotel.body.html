How to choose a 5-star hotel (beyond the photos)

The star rating measures technical criteria — not necessarily whether that hotel is the right one for you.

Two properties can carry the same 5-star rating and offer completely different experiences. The star category tends to assess objective items — room size, number of services, infrastructure — but says nothing about location, clientele, style, or whether that hotel actually fits the reason for your trip.

Location matters more than it seems

An isolated 5-star resort is great for those seeking total rest, but can be frustrating for someone who wants to explore the city. A 5-star hotel in the heart of a bustling capital is perfect for a gastronomy-focused trip, but might lack the tranquility a honeymoon needs. The right question isn't "how many stars" — it's "does this hotel serve the purpose of this trip."

Clientele and property style

Luxury hotels have different personalities: some cater to families, others to couples, others to a younger, livelier crowd. It's worth knowing the predominant guest profile before booking — especially at all-inclusive resorts, like the ones we recommend for Jamaica or Xcaret.
