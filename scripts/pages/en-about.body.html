Our story

From Annestour to ATF Travel

ATF Travel is the evolution of Annestour, rebuilt to bring together, under one identity, highly specialized areas: luxury travel, custom itineraries and end-to-end travel concierge.

The brand is led by Anne Ferri Bernardino and Fernando Nunes, founding partners who combined consolidated business experience with curated stays, destinations and luxury experiences — plus technical expertise in snow travel — to shape a new standard of service in high-end tourism.

Trained in Foreign Trade, Gastronomy and Sommellerie, Anne brings those fields directly into ATF Travel's concept, underpinning wine tourism and fine dining itineraries across Europe and other reference destinations. Fernando complements the agency with curated luxury stays and destinations, the operational capacity to lead groups, and expertise in snow destinations across North America, South America and Europe.
