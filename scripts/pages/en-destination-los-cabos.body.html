Los Cabos

Where the desert meets the sea, at the tip of Mexico's Baja California peninsula.

Why visit Los Cabos

Los Cabos combines dramatic desert landscapes with turquoise beaches, top-tier resorts, and a food scene increasingly recognized internationally. It's a versatile destination, for couples' getaways and family trips alike.
