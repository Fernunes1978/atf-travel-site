Termas de Chillán

Snow and hot springs in the Chilean Andes, a rare combination in South America.

Why visit Termas de Chillán

Termas de Chillán is one of the few destinations in the world that combines skiing and natural hot springs on the same mountain — after a day on the slopes of the Chilean Andes, you can relax in outdoor thermal pools.

ATF Travel builds snow itineraries in South America with curated lodging, ski lessons and access to the hot springs, a more accessible option for those who want the snow experience without crossing hemispheres.
